Utilities, data centres and technology

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
YTL Power (YTLPOWR), YTL Corp (YTL)YTL Power closed at RM4.69 after selling linked to scrutiny of Nvidia; its 51% shareholder YTL also declined. The edition states there were no credible reports linking YTL Power to Megaspeed or Speedmatrix (p5).Separate partner-related sentiment from established wrongdoing. Monitor GPU access, customer commitments, project cash flow and formal company disclosures.
Crescendo (CRESNDO)The Speedmatrix report revisits Crescendo’s RM263.21m land sale to related entity MSFusion. A payment default was subsequently settled, including late fees, according to its September filing (p5).This is background to an existing land sale. Do not treat it as a new award or an unresolved receivable without later evidence.
Zetrix AI (ZETRIX)Confirmed that RM25m nominal sukuk obligations remained unpaid. Bursa required financial, governance and operational disclosures by 11 November; a separate dividend directive carried a 23 November deadline (p15).Prioritise liquidity, dissolution/cross-default terms, JPJ remittances, tax demands, auditor appointment and board independence. Declared dividends are not cash received.
Tenaga Nasional (TENAGA); gas-to-power infrastructureThe minister proposed market-priced gas for dedicated data centre generation. TNB supplies 36 data centres; proposed policy would charge operators for required transmission upgrades and committed capacity (p11).Gas supply is a policy opportunity, subject to existing commitments and infrastructure. No new listed-company gas contract was identified. Check capex funding and tariff recovery.

Further research

  • Compare the issuer filings, sukuk notices and dividend notices with the edition’s account. Bursa company announcements
  • Broker context in the edition. The p5 fund-manager comments express conditional views on YTL Power; they do not establish that allegations are proven or that a rebound will occur.
  • Follow gas access, generation licensing and tariff frameworks. Energy Commission

Construction, engineering and industrial investment

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Favelle Favco (FAVCO)Australian unit received RM83.7m of tower-crane orders, with deliveries due in Q1 2027 and by end-2027 (p6).Track order conversion, margins, currency exposure and collection; contract value is not immediate profit.
Jati Tinggi (JTGROUP)Won RM33.1m of power infrastructure works for a Negeri Sembilan data centre, to run for nine months. The story also recalls a RM2.9m storage-system JV award from TNB (p9).Assess the new job’s working-capital needs and power-cable execution. The earlier JV’s full value should not be assumed attributable to Jati Tinggi.
Critical Holdings (CHB)Secured RM65m of cleanroom EPC contracts for an unnamed multinational, scheduled for completion by 31 March 2027 (p9).Monitor customer concentration and delivery capacity alongside its previously announced Kulim commitments.
Advancecon (ADVCON)Received a third IBTEC earthworks/infrastructure award, worth RM41.52m, from JLG Technopark (p9).Follow project cash collection, equipment utilisation and earthworks margins; distinguish the new award from earlier IBTEC contracts.
Kitacon (KITACON), Sime Darby Property (SIMEPROP)Kitacon accepted RM54.872m of Elmina factory construction from Sime Darby Property’s unit. Work starts 30 November and runs for 22 months (p10).Kitacon gains backlog; Sime Darby Property is the project developer/customer. Assess industrial demand, construction costs and recognition timing.

Further research

  • Read each letter-of-award announcement for scope, duration, conditions and funding. Bursa company announcements
  • Use actual industrial output to test the demand backdrop rather than equating announced investment with contractor revenue. DOSM

Banking, property and REITs

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Public Bank (PBBANK)Shares fell after management indicated full-year ROE nearer 12%, versus earlier guidance of up to 13%, and NIM compression of up to 10bp. A RM3.5bn capital-return plan remained intact in the coverage (p6).Track deposit competition, loan impairment and bond-portfolio sensitivity. Capital-return expectations need formal timing and entitlement confirmation.
Sunway REIT (SUNREIT)Q3 NPI fell 4% to RM173.8m. Distributable income fell to RM116.1m or 3.39 sen/unit; no distribution was declared. Retail improved while hotels weakened and a campus disposal reduced revenue (p13).Separate portfolio disposal effects from same-property performance. Watch hotel occupancy, cost growth, rental reversions and distributions actually declared.
Residential financing and developersJLL cited September housing-loan approval rates of 38.36%, tighter checks on valuations and concern over high-density serviced-apartment vacancies (p16).Conditional exposure: listed developers and mortgage lenders. Compare project-level affordability, loan conversion, unsold stock and buyer quality; no automatic sector beneficiary.

Further research

  • Broker context in the edition. CIMB links Public Bank’s revisions to funding pressure; HLIB cites loan growth and potential capital returns (p6). Test both against subsequent results.
  • Check reported interest margins, credit costs, REIT results and formal capital-return notices. Bursa company announcements

Energy, plantations and corporate actions

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Yinson (YINSON), MISC (MISC)Yinson clarified it had not accepted the proposed RM2.50/share privatisation. MISC, the Lim family and EPF proposed RM4.03bn for the remaining 55.2%; Yinson’s response was due by 5pm on 4 December unless extended (p12).A receipt acknowledgement was not board acceptance. Monitor independent advice, financing, approvals and scheme conditions; assess MISC’s funding burden separately.
Mentiga (MENTIGA)HIS TIZA completed its RM21m purchase of 30.47% from Amanah Saham Pahang, which retained 43.91%. Potential plantation consolidation remains subject to approvals (p10).A shareholder change does not itself inject plantation assets. Evaluate any later transaction price, related-party terms and dilution.
Palm-oil inventory and plantation exposureOctober stocks reached a record 3.62m tonnes, up 3.15% month on month, despite exports rebounding to 1.71m tonnes. Production rose to 2.15m tonnes (p17).Conditional exposure: planters including SD Guthrie and IOI Corp. High stocks can pressure CPO prices; compare company output, realised prices and downstream margins before inferring earnings.

Further research

  • Broker context in the edition. CIMB favours the proposed Yinson exit on its leverage and valuation assumptions; other analysts’ targets differ (p12). This is analyst opinion, not scheme approval.
  • Track stocks, exports and production together. MPOB industry statistics

Other investment-relevant developments

Manufacturing and automationSeptember industrial output rose 6.3% year on year and October PMI returned above 50 (p11).Conditional candidates: automation, testing and cleanroom suppliers such as Pentamaster and CHB. Modular housing and weather disruptionThe issue discusses modular construction and a 70% IBS requirement for major public infrastructure projects of RM100m and above. Tariff-driven order relocation is conditional.

Global spending headlines do not establish local awards.

No direct incremental Bursa gold-custody beneficiary was established; distinguish bullion prices from jewellery retail or mining earnings.
