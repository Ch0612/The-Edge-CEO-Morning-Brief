Healthcare, insurance and pharmacy retail

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Allianz Malaysia (ALLIANZ), Takaful Malaysia (TAKAFUL); MediAsasThe government reported interest from four major hospital groups in MediAsas, targeting a late-January 2027 national rollout. Allianz Life and Takaful Malaysia were among pilot insurers/takaful operators. Indicative monthly premiums were RM60–550 (p3).Final products, pricing and hospital participation were not settled. Assess claims experience, distribution and diagnosis-related-group reimbursement rather than assuming higher margins.
Private hospitals: conditional sector exposureThe hospital groups expressing interest were not named. MediAsas aims to broaden affordable access using standardised benefits and diagnosis-based payment (p3).Inferred candidates: IHH Healthcare and KPJ Healthcare may warrant monitoring, but this edition does not confirm that either is among the four interested groups. Patient volume benefits must be weighed against reimbursement rates.
Big Caring (proposed listing)BIMB highlighted institutional demand but also a demanding RM1.08 IPO valuation, reported as 42 times trailing earnings. Longer-term growth may require expansion beyond pharmacy retail (p13).Broker forecasts and targets are opinions. Evaluate store economics, like-for-like sales and acquisition execution; cornerstone demand does not establish fair value.

Further research

  • Broker context in the edition. BIMB’s Big Caring assessment depends on several years of earnings growth and eventual business expansion (p13). Compare the assumptions with the prospectus.
  • Confirm pilot participation and final scheme terms through official notices, and listed companies’ disclosures. BNM · Bursa company announcements

Construction, electrical infrastructure and industrial investment

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Southern Score (SSB8)Its 51%-owned SJEE unit secured RM146.8m of electrical subcontracts for two Port Dickson data centres, with phased completion dates from October 2027 to April 2028 (p6).The value is for the subsidiary’s contracts, not a direct measure of parent-attributable profit. Track costs, minority interests, concentration and working capital.
SKA Capital (SKACAP); Tenaga Nasional (TENAGA)SKA closed its ACE debut at 49.5 sen against a 43-sen IPO price. Its RM313m order book was split roughly equally between data centres and TNB work (p9).Distinguish primary proceeds for expansion from shareholder sale proceeds. Monitor tender conversion, performance bonds and execution capacity; the tender pipeline is not backlog.
Mitsui Kinzoku copper-foil investmentThe Japanese group plans RM4.87bn of Malaysian plant investment, with operations starting in phases from April 2031 (p6).Potential demand for construction, utilities and semiconductor infrastructure is conditional. No Bursa-listed award recipient was named, and the long lead time matters.
L&P Global (L&PBHD), Taghill (TAGHILL), Nestcon (NESTCON)L&P proposed FMCG and construction diversification after its FY2025 loss. Conditional subcontracts totalled RM11.76m: RM3.5m from Taghill’s unit and RM8.26m from Nestcon’s unit. FMCG arrangements were MOUs (pp11–12).Shareholder/regulatory approvals and formal awards were still needed. Assess management expertise, margins, related commitments and cash requirements; MOUs do not establish revenue.

Further research

  • Compare award conditions, subsidiary ownership and the prospectus use of funds. Bursa company announcements
  • For industrial-investment spillovers, require named tenders or awards before attributing revenue to a listed contractor.

Energy, utilities and plantations

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Dialog (DIALOG)Salbiah achieved first gas on 27 October. Dialog reached final investment decision for Fatimah and plans another US$211m, approximately RM860m, of development investment (p6).Assess production ramp-up, PSC economics, capex commitments, borrowing and cash flow; the funding mix was not finalised.
United Plantations (UTDPLT)Q3 profit reached RM290.93m, up 43.1%, on stronger production and firm prices. It declared 30 sen interim plus 15 sen special dividend, payable 8 December (p7).Monitor yields, realised prices and downstream hedge timing. A special dividend should not be treated as a recurring annual payout.
YTL Power (YTLPOWR)CGS argued the sell-off was excessive, distinguishing contracted colocation capacity of 298MW from its larger 850MW valuation assumption and broader AI ambitions (p13).The assumed capacity is not existing contracted capacity. Test power supply, gas curtailment, tenant demand and regulatory risk rather than adopting a broker target as fact.
Palm-oil supply and export dutyAnalysts forecast inventory drawdown from Q1 2027 after October’s record 3.62m tonnes. December’s CPO reference price was cut to RM4,259.70/tonne, leaving export duty at 10% (p10).Forecast drawdown depends on weather, output, exports and biodiesel demand. A possible duty waiver was discussed but not implemented in this edition.
PETROS and Samalaju industrial gasPETROS agreed gas supply for OCI TerraSus’ Bintulu polysilicon operations (p10).The named parties are not Bursa-listed equity award recipients. Possible broader infrastructure benefits require disclosed contracts.

Further research

  • Broker context in the edition. CGS and HLIB outline different near-term inventory drivers (p10); CGS’s YTL Power case relies on colocation assumptions (p13). Separate forecasts from contracted operations.
  • Track production, exports and duty notices. MPOB
  • Check upstream capex and first-gas disclosures. Bursa company announcements

Consumer, property and new listings

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Heineken Malaysia (HEIM)Q3 profit fell 38.74% to RM69.18m and revenue fell 17.82% to RM539.14m. Lower volumes reflected weak sentiment, higher excise duty and inventory normalisation, expected to conclude in Q4 (p7).Watch sell-through volumes, affordability, pricing and costs. Destocking completion is management’s expectation, not proof of a demand recovery; no quarterly dividend was declared.
Empire Premium Food (EMPIRE)Q2 FY2027 profit was RM13.69m on RM85.23m revenue; profit rose 26.2% quarter on quarter with nearly flat sales. Declared 0.67 sen dividend, payable 8 December; plans 165 outlets by March 2027 (p8).Compare margin improvements with same-store sales and expansion costs. No year-on-year comparison was provided for this newly listed company.
S P Setia (SPSETIA)Q3 profit fell 52.06% to RM32.61m, with lower revenue, no comparable land sale and higher costs. Net gearing improved to 0.30 times; nine-month sales reached RM2.34bn (p8).Separate recurring development profit from land sales. Track cash conversion, launches and overseas delivery; lower gearing alone does not reverse weaker earnings.
Panda Eyes (proposed ACE listing)Signed an underwriting agreement with M&A Securities for a planned January 2027 IPO, with 15 additional stores proposed (p9).Pricing and final prospectus terms matter. Evaluate store returns, online-sales costs and use of proceeds before inferring listing upside.

Further research

  • Compare recurring earnings, balance sheets, formal dividend notices and final prospectuses. Bursa company announcements

Technology and governance

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Zetrix AI (ZETRIX)Bursa extended its financial/governance disclosure deadline to 13 November. Dividend payments to certain affected holders remained withheld; the issue also recounts the sukuk default, RM314m JPJ remittances and a unit’s RM353.13m additional tax assessment (p11).An extension does not resolve funding, payment or governance deficiencies. Watch actual remittances, sukukholder remedies, tax proceedings, auditor appointment and complete disclosures.
