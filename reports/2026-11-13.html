Technology, data centres and governance

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
YTL Power (YTLPOWR), YTL Corp (YTL)The edition reports Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s departure and a fresh AI sell-off. YTL Power fell over 8% to RM4.40 and YTL fell 5.56% to RM1.87 in the preceding session (pp2,4,21).These are edition-reported developments and historical prices. Assess partner continuity, GPU supply and tenant demand through formal disclosures; partner scrutiny does not establish wrongdoing by YTL.
NationGate (NATGATE)The Nvidia-linked manufacturer fell 6.42% to RM1.75 in the data centre sell-off. Separately, it was the sole Malaysian small-cap index addition reported in the MSCI review (pp4,11).Order/customer concentration and possible index buying are different drivers. Confirm the official rebalance date and assess actual orders, margins and cash conversion.
Gamuda (GAMUDA), Sunway Construction (SUNCON), IJM (IJM), Mah Sing (MAHSING), Sime Darby Property (SIMEPROP)These construction/property exposures fell with the data centre trade; reported declines ranged from 0.78% for Sime Darby Property to 6.63% for Mah Sing (p4).A share-price fall does not mean a contract was cancelled. Separate contracted backlog, speculative land demand, customer financing and project timing.
Zetrix AI (ZETRIX)AmanahRaya Investment Management marked its Zetrix sukuk holdings across four funds to nil after the missed RM25.68m principal-plus-profit payment. Recovery rights remained; dealings in the four funds were suspended until 23 November (p3).Nil valuation reflects uncertainty, not a confirmed final recovery rate. Monitor acceleration/dissolution remedies, cash resources, creditor priority and complete financial disclosures. The article reports a RM2bn programme, not that all of it is necessarily outstanding.
Data centre regulation and export controlsMalaysia was preparing tighter operator/GPU disclosures and would review possible re-export breaches by Speedmatrix. A separate infrastructure bill was also being developed (p7).For Bursa exposures, monitor compliance costs, end-user checks, chip access and power/water permits. Proposed laws are not enacted requirements; no finding against a listed company is established here.

Further research

  • Check issuer announcements for project changes, material customer exposure, sukuk obligations and regulatory responses. Bursa company announcements
  • Compare official additions, deletions and effective dates. MSCI index reviews
  • Source-date caution. The p11 MSCI article prints an effective year of 2025 in this 2026 edition. Do not use that apparent typo as a trading date.

Healthcare and pharmaceuticals

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Pharmaniaga (PHARMA)Q3 profit rose to RM26.27m from RM7.28m, supported by a RM16.41m tax credit and lower financing costs, while revenue grew 0.72%. Declared 0.32 sen interim dividend, payable 20 January 2027 (pp10–11).Separate recurring operating growth from tax effects and refinancing savings. Confirm payout notices rather than repeating the article’s inconsistent year-to-date dividend unit.
Pharmaniaga: product pipelineThe group plans nine product launches in 2027 and reported approval for ISENDA (liraglutide) and EVnyx (EV71 vaccine). It expects the concession purchase list to expand over the concession period (pp10–11).Regulatory approval does not establish sales. Track launch timing, procurement uptake, pricing, manufacturing costs and recurring cash generation.
Hartalega (HARTA)Q2 FY2027 profit rose nearly 80% to RM32.8m as higher selling prices helped offset raw-material costs. Six-month profit reached RM102.83m; no quarterly dividend was declared (p14).Compare quarter-on-quarter trends as well as the low prior-year base. Watch ASP/input-cost spreads, ringgit exposure, utilisation, recommissioning capex and global excess capacity.

Further research

  • Compare tax, finance costs and operating cash flow with reported profit; track product launches and glove pricing in subsequent filings. Bursa company announcements
  • Treat US tariff benefits for Malaysian gloves as conditional on actual customer volumes and sustainable margins, as the edition also highlights competition outside the US.

Energy, utilities and plantations

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
Tenaga Nasional (TENAGA)Expanded household bill protection was estimated at RM248m across four months. TNB shares September AFA costs, bears the October–December AFA cost and absorbs RM36m of retail charges; the government bears the SST exemption (p16).Do not attribute the full RM248m to TNB. Track the exact cost split, accounting treatment, fuel recovery and the separate review of the AFA mechanism for 2027.
Keyfield (KEYFIELD)Q3 profit was almost flat at RM41.4m despite revenue rising 30.3% and utilisation reaching 89%. Lower charter rates, vessel mix and depreciation compressed margins. Declared two sen dividend, payable 8 January (p12).Higher utilisation can coexist with weak returns. Assess net rates, owned versus third-party vessel economics, depreciation and capex; anticipated 2027–28 activity is not contracted revenue.
Johor Plantations (JPG)Q3 profit fell 5.4% to RM100.26m as lower deliveries outweighed firmer prices. Declared 1.5 sen dividend, payable 10 December; nine-month profit fell 21.5% (p12).Track delivery timing, output, realised prices and inventories. Management expects stock levels to limit near-term CPO upside.
Rimbunan Sawit (RSAWIT)Proposed disposing of 613 hectares for RM18.39m cash, approximately 2.17% above valuation, to a Sarawak charitable trust. The parcel’s audited book value was RM1.13m (p14).Assess completion conditions, taxes and disposal costs before estimating any gain. A disposal gain is distinct from recurring plantation earnings.
CCUS and green investmentMalaysia targets a CCUS roadmap by end-November. Storage assessment permits and Kerteh studies were discussed, while green-project developers were urged to demonstrate commercial viability (p9).Conditional exposure: engineering, offshore and gas-service providers such as Dialog. No listed CCUS award was identified. Storage availability, tariffs, offtake and financing determine viability.

Further research

  • Monitor official tariff/AFA decisions and cost-allocation mechanisms. Energy Commission
  • Compare estate production, deliveries and stocks. MPOB
  • Use subsequent issuer filings to check vessel rates, disposal completion and firm CCUS commitments. Bursa company announcements

Consumer, aviation and property

Company / investment themeNewsInvestment considerations
MR DIY (MRDIY)Shares closed at RM1.05 amid concern over Pinduoduo competition. Most brokers remained positive; Q3 results were scheduled for 17 November (p4).Broker targets and temporary-subsidy arguments need testing against sales, traffic, basket size and margins. A lower earnings multiple does not by itself establish value.
AirAsia Group (AAGB); aviation capacity and supportMalaysia Airlines called for equal treatment if support were offered to AirAsia, while the edition states no government financial-aid commitment had been agreed. Malaysia Airlines also cited delayed aircraft deliveries (p10).Track official support terms, dilution, funding and competitive capacity. Malaysia Airlines is not a Bursa-listed equity candidate; its delivery delays may alter competition without guaranteeing AirAsia gains.
Eco World (ECOWLD)Signed a one-year MOU with NS Corporation to study desalination/industrial water supply for Negeri Sembilan industrial parks (p8).This is a feasibility collaboration, not a construction award. Assess water costs, environmental approval, capex, offtake and funding before modelling income.
Residential developers and unsold stockThe ministry proposed a housing data repository. Completed unsold homes rose to 33,094 units worth RM17.8bn in Q2 2026 (p15).Potential lower compliance costs remain a policy objective. Monitor locality-level demand, pricing and inventory rather than assuming a broad developer rerating.

Further research

  • Broker context in the edition. CGS argues MR DIY’s competitive headwind may prove temporary; Kenanga highlights household support (p4). Compare these views with the forthcoming results.
  • Read company results, funding terms and Eco World’s MOU conditions. Bursa company announcements

MSCI flows across Bursa sectorsThe review reports small-cap deletions of AEON Credit, AirAsia Group, KSL, Mah Sing, Matrix Concepts, Takaful Malaysia and Zetrix; the main Malaysia index was unchanged (p11).Passive selling can affect liquidity independently of business performance. Taiwan trade and semiconductor investmentMalaysia requested resumption of informal economic discussions. The PM said trade and investment flows had not been disrupted and cited strong nine-month trade growth (p8).Conditional exposure: semiconductor and electronics suppliers. Track actual investment decisions and customer orders, not diplomatic statements alone.
AI spending versus execution constraintsGlobal coverage contrasts Nvidia turmoil and possible remote-compute restrictions with Foxconn’s strong demand outlook, Goldman’s capex forecasts, Australian permit delays and Alphabet fundraising (pp21–23,26,29).Demand forecasts can coexist with funding, component and approval bottlenecks. Do not translate global capex forecasts into guaranteed orders for local manufacturers or contractors.
Rates, gold, oil and regional riskGold extended losses below US$4,000 amid higher-rate expectations; the issue also covers Indonesian index/political risks, weaker Thai tourism and renewed tariff threats (pp24,27–30).Banks, REITs, exporters and airlines face different rate, FX and demand effects. No direct Bursa beneficiary of regional outflows or tourism diversion was established.
Defence procurement and ageing servicesMalaysia preferred a Turkish alternative for the LCS missile programme, subject to negotiations; a dedicated senior-citizens ministry was proposed (pp15–16).No new Bursa-listed defence award was named. Healthcare, accessible housing and eldercare demand remain long-term themes requiring funding and viable economics.

Further research
