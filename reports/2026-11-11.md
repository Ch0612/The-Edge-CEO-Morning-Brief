## Utilities, data centres and technology

| Company / investment theme | News | Investment considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **YTL Power (YTLPOWR), YTL Corp (YTL)** | YTL Power closed at RM4.69 after selling linked to scrutiny of Nvidia; its 51% shareholder YTL also declined. The edition states there were no credible reports linking YTL Power to Megaspeed or Speedmatrix (p5). | Separate partner-related sentiment from established wrongdoing. Monitor GPU access, customer commitments, project cash flow and formal company disclosures. |
| **Crescendo (CRESNDO)** | The Speedmatrix report revisits Crescendo’s RM263.21m land sale to related entity MSFusion. A payment default was subsequently settled, including late fees, according to its September filing (p5). | This is background to an existing land sale. Do not treat it as a new award or an unresolved receivable without later evidence. |
| **Zetrix AI (ZETRIX)** | Confirmed that RM25m nominal sukuk obligations remained unpaid. Bursa required financial, governance and operational disclosures by 11 November; a separate dividend directive carried a 23 November deadline (p15). | Prioritise liquidity, dissolution/cross-default terms, JPJ remittances, tax demands, auditor appointment and board independence. Declared dividends are not cash received. |
| **Tenaga Nasional (TENAGA); gas-to-power infrastructure** | The minister proposed market-priced gas for dedicated data centre generation. TNB supplies 36 data centres; proposed policy would charge operators for required transmission upgrades and committed capacity (p11). | Gas supply is a policy opportunity, subject to existing commitments and infrastructure. No new listed-company gas contract was identified. Check capex funding and tariff recovery. |

### Further research

- Compare the issuer filings, sukuk notices and dividend notices with the edition’s account. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)
- **Broker context in the edition.** The p5 fund-manager comments express conditional views on YTL Power; they do not establish that allegations are proven or that a rebound will occur.
- Follow gas access, generation licensing and tariff frameworks. [Energy Commission](https://www.st.gov.my/)

## Banking, property and REITs

| Company / investment theme | News | Investment considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **Public Bank (PBBANK)** | Shares fell after management indicated full-year ROE nearer 12%, versus earlier guidance of up to 13%, and NIM compression of up to 10bp. A RM3.5bn capital-return plan remained intact in the coverage (p6). | Track deposit competition, loan impairment and bond-portfolio sensitivity. | **Sunway REIT (SUNREIT)** | Q3 NPI fell 4% to RM173.8m. Distributable income fell to RM116.1m or 3.39 sen/unit; no distribution was declared. Retail improved while hotels weakened and a campus disposal reduced revenue (p13). | Separate portfolio disposal effects from same-property performance. Watch hotel occupancy, cost growth, rental reversions and distributions actually declared. |

Compare project-level affordability, loan conversion, unsold stock and buyer quality; no automatic sector beneficiary. |

### Further research

- **Broker context in the edition.** CIMB links Public Bank’s revisions to funding pressure; HLIB cites loan growth and potential capital returns (p6). Test both against subsequent results.
- Check reported interest margins, credit costs, REIT results and formal capital-return notices. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)

## Energy, plantations and corporate actions

| **Yinson (YINSON), MISC (MISC)** | Yinson clarified it had not accepted the proposed RM2.50/share privatisation. Potential plantation consolidation remains subject to approvals (p10). | A shareholder change does not itself inject plantation assets. Production rose to 2.15m tonnes (p17). | Conditional exposure: planters including SD Guthrie and IOI Corp. High stocks can pressure CPO prices; compare company output, realised prices and downstream margins before inferring earnings. |

### Further research

- **Broker context in the edition.** CIMB favours the proposed Yinson exit on its leverage and valuation assumptions; other analysts’ targets differ (p12). This is analyst opinion, not scheme approval.
- Track stocks, exports and production together. [MPOB industry statistics](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/)
