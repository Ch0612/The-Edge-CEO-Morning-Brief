Construction and industrial investment

Sunway Construction (SUNCON) and parent Sunway (SUNWAY)SUNCON secured RM1.74bn for a Bandar Serendah data centre, lifting outstanding orders to RM13.4bn. Completion is targeted for Q4 2028 (p6).Track margins, execution and cash collection alongside hyperscaler concentration.
Tanco (TANCO)Its consortium with CNECC Engineering secured RM700m of Klang warehouse works (p6).The headline value is for the consortium. Tanco earns an undisclosed coordination and management fee, while its partner bears execution costs and risks.
Industrial logisticsDaiso opened its RM1.2bn Pulau Indah logistics hub (p9).Watch throughput, supplier contracts and whether the development attracts follow-on investment. The headline value is not a listed-company order.

Kobay Technology (KOBAY)Q1 FY2027 profit rose 73% to RM18.66m on RM130.24m revenue (p13).Assess the segment mix and whether stronger profit translates into operating cash flow.
Inari (INARI) and Malaysian Pacific Industries (MPI)Both fell during a technology sell-off. Research cited ongoing global AI spending as a counterweight (p12).Treat sector commentary separately from issuer earnings. Track actual customer order trends.
