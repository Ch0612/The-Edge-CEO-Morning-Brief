What matters most

  • Power and infrastructure: Tenaga and IOI Corp plan a 500MW solar project with battery storage; Malakoff has restarted 429MW of gas capacity. GDB’s new logistics-hub contract adds to construction visibility.
  • Property monetisation: IOI Properties is pursuing the Malaysian REIT listing and a Singapore private property fund. The cash proceeds, debt reduction and earnings transferred out need to be assessed together.
  • Plantation pressure: The edition reports a sharp palm-oil inventory build-up and difficulties for independent mills. A proposed export-duty waiver remains a proposal, not an approved catalyst.
  • AI has two sides: MM Computer Systems has an actual server order, while the Perak data-centre proposal is only at a preliminary stage. Reported restrictions on Chinese customers could complicate future GPU-cloud demand.
  • Oil volatility: Energy shares weakened on peace-deal expectations, despite subsequent reports of further strikes. Separate businesses exposed to oil prices from contracted storage, shipping and utility earnings.
