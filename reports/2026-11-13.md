## Technology, data centres and governance

| Company / investment theme | News | Investment considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **YTL Power (YTLPOWR), YTL Corp (YTL)** | The edition reports Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s departure and a fresh AI sell-off. YTL Power fell over 8% to RM4.40 and YTL fell 5.56% to RM1.87 in the preceding session (pp2,4,21). | These are edition-reported developments and historical prices. Assess partner continuity, GPU supply and tenant demand through formal disclosures; partner scrutiny does not establish wrongdoing by YTL. |
| **NationGate (NATGATE)** | The Nvidia-linked manufacturer fell 6.42% to RM1.75 in the data centre sell-off. Separately, it was the sole Malaysian small-cap index addition reported in the MSCI review (pp4,11). | Order/customer concentration and possible index buying are different drivers. Confirm the official rebalance date and assess actual orders, margins and cash conversion. |
| **Gamuda (GAMUDA), Sunway Construction (SUNCON), IJM (IJM), Mah Sing (MAHSING), Sime Darby Property (SIMEPROP)** | These construction/property exposures fell with the data centre trade; reported declines ranged from 0.78% for Sime Darby Property to 6.63% for Mah Sing (p4). | A share-price fall does not mean a contract was cancelled. Separate contracted backlog, speculative land demand, customer financing and project timing. |
| **Zetrix AI (ZETRIX)** | AmanahRaya Investment Management marked its Zetrix sukuk holdings across four funds to nil after the missed RM25.68m principal-plus-profit payment. Recovery rights remained; dealings in the four funds were suspended until 23 November (p3). | Nil valuation reflects uncertainty, not a confirmed final recovery rate. Monitor acceleration/dissolution remedies, cash resources, creditor priority and complete financial disclosures. The article reports a RM2bn programme, not that all of it is necessarily outstanding. |
| **Data centre regulation and export controls** | Malaysia was preparing tighter operator/GPU disclosures and would review possible re-export breaches by Speedmatrix. A separate infrastructure bill was also being developed (p7). | For Bursa exposures, monitor compliance costs, end-user checks, chip access and power/water permits. Proposed laws are not enacted requirements; no finding against a listed company is established here. |

### Further research

- Check issuer announcements for project changes, material customer exposure, sukuk obligations and regulatory responses. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)
- Compare official additions, deletions and effective dates. [MSCI index reviews](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index-resources/index-review)
- **Source-date caution.** The p11 MSCI article prints an effective year of 2025 in this 2026 edition. Do not use that apparent typo as a trading date.

Watch ASP/input-cost spreads, ringgit exposure, utilisation, recommissioning capex and global excess capacity. |

- Compare tax, finance costs and operating cash flow with reported profit; track product launches and glove pricing in subsequent filings. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)
- Treat US tariff benefits for Malaysian gloves as conditional on actual customer volumes and sustainable margins, as the edition also highlights competition outside the US.

## Energy, utilities and plantations

| Company / investment theme | News | Investment considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **Tenaga Nasional (TENAGA)** | Expanded household bill protection was estimated at RM248m across four months. TNB shares September AFA costs, bears the October–December AFA cost and absorbs RM36m of retail charges; the government bears the SST exemption (p16). | Do not attribute the full RM248m to TNB. Track the exact cost split, accounting treatment, fuel recovery and the separate review of the AFA mechanism for 2027. |
| **Keyfield (KEYFIELD)** | Q3 profit was almost flat at RM41.4m despite revenue rising 30.3% and utilisation reaching 89%. Lower charter rates, vessel mix and depreciation compressed margins. Declared two sen dividend, payable 8 January (p12). | Higher utilisation can coexist with weak returns. Assess net rates, owned versus third-party vessel economics, depreciation and capex; anticipated 2027–28 activity is not contracted revenue. |
| **Johor Plantations (JPG)** | Q3 profit fell 5.4% to RM100.26m as lower deliveries outweighed firmer prices. Declared 1.5 sen dividend, payable 10 December; nine-month profit fell 21.5% (p12). | Track delivery timing, output, realised prices and inventories. Management expects stock levels to limit near-term CPO upside. |
| **Rimbunan Sawit (RSAWIT)** | Proposed disposing of 613 hectares for RM18.39m cash, approximately 2.17% above valuation, to a Sarawak charitable trust. The parcel’s audited book value was RM1.13m (p14). | Assess completion conditions, taxes and disposal costs before estimating any gain. A disposal gain is distinct from recurring plantation earnings. |
| **CCUS and green investment** | Malaysia targets a CCUS roadmap by end-November. Storage assessment permits and Kerteh studies were discussed, while green-project developers were urged to demonstrate commercial viability (p9). | **Conditional exposure:** engineering, offshore and gas-service providers such as Dialog. No listed CCUS award was identified. Storage availability, tariffs, offtake and financing determine viability. |

### Further research

- Monitor official tariff/AFA decisions and cost-allocation mechanisms. [Energy Commission](https://www.st.gov.my/)
- Compare estate production, deliveries and stocks. [MPOB](https://bepi.mpob.gov.my/)
- Use subsequent issuer filings to check vessel rates, disposal completion and firm CCUS commitments. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)

## Consumer, aviation and property

| **MR DIY (MRDIY)** | Shares closed at RM1.05 amid concern over Pinduoduo competition. Most brokers remained positive; Q3 results were scheduled for 17 November (p4). | Broker targets and temporary-subsidy arguments need testing against sales, traffic, basket size and margins. Assess water costs, environmental approval, capex, offtake and funding before modelling income. |
| **Residential developers and unsold stock** | The ministry proposed a housing data repository. Completed unsold homes rose to 33,094 units worth RM17.8bn in Q2 2026 (p15). | Potential lower compliance costs remain a policy objective. Monitor locality-level demand, pricing and inventory rather than assuming a broad developer rerating. |

### Further research

- **Broker context in the edition.** CGS argues MR DIY’s competitive headwind may prove temporary; Kenanga highlights household support (p4). Compare these views with the forthcoming results.
- Read company results, funding terms and Eco World’s MOU conditions. [Bursa company announcements](https://www.bursamalaysia.com/market_information/announcements/company_announcement)
