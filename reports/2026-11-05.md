## Other investment-relevant developments

| Investment theme | News | Potential Bursa beneficiaries & considerations |

MediAsas gained an independent oversight board and was targeting a pilot at end-January 2027 (pp11,14). | Fuel relief depends on hedging and pass-through. Health-provider and insurer implications depend on participation and reimbursement terms. |
| **Global policy and demand** | The US election shifted congressional control, lifting renewable shares while executives saw little immediate tariff relief. China expanded stock-support measures. BMW’s profit warning and AI/cybersecurity news showed uneven demand and spending (pp3,20–23,26–27). | Monitor US policy implementation, end-market orders and risk appetite. Overseas stock moves are insufficient evidence of a Bursa earnings benefit. |
